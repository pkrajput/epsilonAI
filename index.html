Overview

Features.

GitTRACE replaces the public forge with a local, trace-recording git. And it turns that trace into a high-level view of your whole product.

Git-like version control that runs on your machines and records a trace of every change.

  • Every step stores the written reasoning behind it
  • Your code is never uploaded, team sync travels encrypted

Why

Public code is training data now.

Crawlers hit public repos around the clock. Whatever you push gets scraped and ends up in someone's training set. The reporting on this is extensive.

The relay never sees your code.

A push archives the trace on your machine and encrypts it to every teammate's SSH public key before anything leaves the device. The relay stores ciphertext and nothing else.

Decryption needs a private key, and private keys never leave members' laptops. A full dump of the server yields password hashes, public keys and encrypted blobs.

The scheme is the audited age format with ChaCha20-Poly1305. Every push becomes a new version, so older states stay pullable and conflicts are detected instead of overwritten.

        Part 2
        
The decision ledger, in plain words.

        
Development is full of attempts that go nowhere. The ledger keeps them. Every step gets a verdict, survived, revised or abandoned, worked out from the commit record itself. Nobody has to remember to write anything down.

        
Six months later someone asks why payments.py retries a failed charge instead of failing fast. The answer is one command away, with the dead first attempt still attached.

        
The same verdicts show where bloat comes from. Code whose steps kept getting abandoned or rewritten stands out, so you can see what never earned its place in the codebase.
